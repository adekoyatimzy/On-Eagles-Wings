About Us

Discover our journey, our vision in Basildon, and what drives our church community.

RCCG On Eagles’ Wings Basildon is a vibrant parish of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, committed to raising a Christ‑centered community where individuals and families can grow spiritually, discover purpose, and thrive in God’s presence. We are a welcoming church for all, rooted in prayer, worship, and the teaching of God’s Word. Our desire is to see lives transformed, and people empowered to soar on the wings of faith.

Our Leadership

Meet Our Pastor

Pastor Onanuga serves as the resident Pastor of RCCG On Eagles’ Wings Basildon. He is a dedicated servant of God with a deep passion for teaching the Word, nurturing spiritual growth, and building a strong, loving church community. With a heart for people and a commitment to excellence in ministry, Pastor Onanuga leads the church with humility, wisdom, and prayer.

Mission, Vision and Goal

  • To make Heaven
  • To take as many people with us
  • Connect people to the Lord
  • Foster positive relationship among our members.
  • To impact communities through love, service and evangelism
  • To help individuals discover their God given purpose and reach their maximum potential
